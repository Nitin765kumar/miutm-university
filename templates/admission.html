Keep these ready

You only type your details online. Documents are checked in person at counselling, so nothing is uploaded.

  • Class 10 and class 12 marksheets
  • Degree or diploma marksheets, for postgraduate and diploma programmes
  • A photo identity document
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Category certificate, if you claim a reserved seat
  • Transfer or migration certificate from your last institution

Fees and scholarships

Each programme page shows its fee. Merit, sports, economically weaker section and girl-child scholarships are available; amounts and eligibility are published with the prospectus.
