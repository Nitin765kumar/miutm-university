Rules for uploads

  • PDF, JPG or PNG only, up to 2 MB per file, one file for each document.
  • Scans must be clear and complete. Unclear documents are sent back with a reason and you can upload them again.
  • A document that has been verified cannot be replaced.
  • Uploads are optional while you apply, but the office cannot finish checking without them.

At counselling

Bring the originals of every document you uploaded, a photo identity document, and passport-size photographs. Admission is confirmed after the originals are verified.
