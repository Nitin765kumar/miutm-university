How admission works

The whole process is online until counselling, when you bring your original documents.

  1. Apply online

    Fill in the form in about ten minutes. You get an application number straight away.

  2. We check your details

    The admission office reviews your marks and eligibility. Follow progress on the tracker.

  3. Merit list

    Selected applicants appear in the merit list on the published date.

  4. Confirm your seat

    Attend counselling with original documents and pay the fee to confirm.
