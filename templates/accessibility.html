Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site.

What we do

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a skip link.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors say what to fix.
  • Text colours meet contrast guidelines, and text can be enlarged without breaking the layout.
  • Images have text descriptions, and animation respects your reduced-motion setting.
  • The application form works without JavaScript.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, write to {{ departments[7].email }} or use the contact form. Say which page and what device or assistive technology you use, and we will fix it.
